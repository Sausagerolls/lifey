# Lifey privacy policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Lifey does not collect any data.

## What the app stores

Lifey saves your life totals, commander damage, counters and table setup on your own device using the system's standard storage (`UserDefaults`). That data never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

## What the app sends

Nothing. Lifey makes no network requests of any kind. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs of any sort.

## What we can see

Nothing about you or your games. We have no server and receive no reports from the app.

Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated figures such as download counts and crash reports through App Store Connect, under Apple's own terms and only if you have agreed to share analytics with Apple. Those figures are not tied to a person and we cannot use them to identify anyone.

## Children

Lifey is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anybody, children included.

## Checking this for yourself

The full source code is public at https://github.com/Sausagerolls/lifey. There is no networking code in it.
